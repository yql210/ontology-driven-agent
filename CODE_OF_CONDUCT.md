# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to participation in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related communications.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel. Do not publish sensitive reports in public project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to reported or observed behavior, including requesting a change in behavior, removing content, or limiting participation.
